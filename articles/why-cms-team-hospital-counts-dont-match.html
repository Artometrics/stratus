Why CMS TEAM Hospital Counts Don’t Match: 741, 721, and the Roster Confusion

Press clips, CMS pages, and internal spreadsheets still disagree on how many hospitals are in TEAM. Here is what each number means — and which figure Rainfall treats as source of truth.

Jennifer Tim-Diamond
· July 22, 2026
TL;DR
  • 721 is Rainfall’s active source of truth
  • 741 is the Sept 2024 public list
  • Closures and merges explain the drop
  • DIY subtractions create 717–720 myths
  • Check CCN and CBSA, not headlines

Why do different sources cite different TEAM hospital counts?

If you have sat in a TEAM steering committee lately, you have probably heard three incompatible sentences in the same hour: “CMS says 721,” “our deck still says 741,” and “someone on LinkedIn said it’s really ~718.” None of those speakers is necessarily lying. They are usually looking at different snapshots of a living participant list — or subtracting hospitals by hand without a published inactive file.

Rainfall Health’s Sandbox map and research hub use 721 active hospitals from the March 2026 CMS TEAM participant update, embedded in territoryHospitals.json. That is the figure we recommend CFOs and compliance teams standardize on unless CMS publishes a newer file.

This explainer walks through each common number, what it actually represents, and how a hospital can verify its own status without trusting a slide deck from last fall.

What does 741 mean?

741 (sometimes rounded in secondary coverage) refers to the September 2024 CMS TEAM participant list that circulated widely after the final rule and early model communications. Many consulting decks, newsletters, and internal trackers froze that number as “the roster.”

That list was real. It is also out of date for operational planning in mid-2026. Hospitals close, merge under a surviving CCN, convert payment status away from IPPS/OPPS combinations required for TEAM, or otherwise leave the active participant set. CMS’s public materials and subsequent updates moved the active count to 721.

What does 721 mean — and why does Rainfall use it?

721 is the active participant count on the March 2026 CMS TEAM update that Rainfall ingested into the territory map. Every state hospital profile, region hub, and Tools page in Sandbox that cites a national total uses this figure.

Territory rollups sum exactly to 721: Territory 1 160 + Territory 2 161 + Territory 3 236 + Territory 4 164. If your internal total does not reconcile to one of those territory sums, you are not looking at the same file.

Where do 720, 718, and 717 come from?

There is no official CMS “720 hospitals” TEAM total in Rainfall’s working files. When people say 720, they are often (a) misremembering 721, (b) confusing TEAM with an HRRP statistic (our HRRP article analyzes 11,720 hospital-condition pairs), or (c) looking at a scraped table that dropped a row.

Tallies around 717–718 usually appear when a consultant or hospital system subtracts “three hospitals that closed” or “a few that merged” from 721 without a CMS-published inactive list. Those exits may be directionally true — closures, mergers, and IPPS-status changes are exactly why 741 fell to 721 — but DIY subtraction is not a new official roster. Until CMS publishes the next file, treat 717/718 as a rumor that must be verified CCN by CCN.

If three additional hospitals leave after March 2026, the correct process is: wait for CMS’s update (or confirm via your CMS Innovation Center contacts), then revise. Do not silently ship a new “official” number into board materials.

How should hospital leaders verify their own status?

  1. Confirm your CCN’s primary address CBSA against the CMS-selected CBSA list — TEAM is geographic, not brand-based.
  2. Check whether you are paid under IPPS (and OPPS as required) — payment-status changes can remove you even if the building still operates.
  3. Reconcile any “741” spreadsheet to the March 2026 721-hospital file hospital-by-hospital.
  4. Use Rainfall’s /territory-map to see the active directory Rainfall uses in research.
  5. Document the vintage of every roster you present to the board (“CMS list as of March 2026”).

Compliance and finance should agree on a single roster owner. Most of the confusion we see is organizational: strategy uses 741, quality uses a consultant PDF, and the CFO uses 721 from a webinar — then the reconciliation meeting becomes theology.

What Rainfall will keep consistent going forward

Rainfall Health site copy, the Tools page, and CMS TEAM hospital profiles standardize on 721 active hospitals (March 2026). Mentions of 741 will be labeled as the September 2024 historical list. Trade-press variants remain attributed, not authoritative.

When CMS publishes the next participant update, Rainfall will revise territoryHospitals.json, territory legend counts, and this explainer’s “source of truth” sentence in the same release.

Frequently asked questions

How many hospitals are in CMS TEAM right now?

Rainfall uses 721 active participants from the March 2026 CMS TEAM update as the current source of truth.

Why do people still say 741?

741 reflects the September 2024 CMS public participant list that many decks froze. Subsequent closures, mergers, and IPPS-status changes reduced the active count to 721.

Is the real number 718?

Not as an official CMS total in our files. Figures near 717–718 usually come from informal subtraction of exits. Verify each CCN against CMS before planning to that number.

What about 720?

720 is not an authoritative TEAM hospital total here. It is often a misremembered 721 or a confusion with unrelated CMS statistics (e.g., HRRP pair counts).

Are Maryland hospitals “missing” from 721?

Maryland is excluded from TEAM because of the Maryland Total Cost of Care Model — that is a design choice, not a roster error.
