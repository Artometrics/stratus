HCA Healthcare Under CMS TEAM: Multi-State Density and the TriStar Playbook

HCA-family brands — including TriStar in Tennessee and HealthONE in Colorado — form the largest for-profit name cluster on the 721-hospital TEAM roster. Here is what competitors and affiliates should learn from that density.

Jennifer Tim-Diamond
· July 22, 2026
TL;DR
  • ~29 HCA-family TEAM sites matched
  • Nashville CBSA is TriStar core
  • Multi-state PAC playbooks scale
  • Competitors must map HCA SNFs
  • Roster vintage still March 2026

How exposed is HCA to CMS TEAM?

On Rainfall’s March 2026 directory, hospital-name patterns associated with HCA — including TriStar, HealthONE, and related cues such as Medical City — match approximately 29 TEAM hospitals, the largest for-profit cluster we measure. Exact legal ownership can differ from signage; treat the count as a density signal, not a 10-K extract.

Concentration is highest in Territory 4 (Tennessee / Florida) with additional Colorado HealthONE presence inside Denver’s 21-hospital TEAM CBSA (Territory 1). That multi-territory spread lets HCA reuse episode playbooks — order sets, PAC contracts, gainsharing templates — across CBSAs.

Why density matters in Nashville and beyond

When multiple sister hospitals sit in one CBSA, discharge destination norms propagate. A TriStar preferred SNF panel becomes the default rehab market even for non-HCA hospitals that lack an alternative. Independent Nashville and Jacksonville hospitals should assume they are price-takers in PAC unless they build competing capacity or joint panels.

See the Tennessee and Florida TEAM hospital directories for named hospitals, and the South region hub for CBSA rollups.

What should non-HCA hospitals do in HCA-heavy markets?

  1. Inventory every SNF and home-health agency that receives your LEJR and fracture patients.
  2. Flag agencies that are de facto HCA-preferred and negotiate dual-panel access or build alternatives.
  3. Mirror HCA’s operational cadence: weekly episode huddles, implant formularies, and early home discharge goals.
  4. Avoid competing on brand advertising; compete on measurable 30-day cost and readmission outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

How many HCA hospitals are in CMS TEAM?

About 29 TEAM hospitals match HCA-family name patterns in Rainfall’s March 2026 directory. Confirm legal ownership at the CCN level for contracting.

Where is HCA densest under TEAM?

Tennessee (TriStar / Nashville), Florida HCA markets, and Colorado HealthONE within Denver’s TEAM CBSA.

Does HCA’s size guarantee TEAM profits?

No, but scaled PAC contracting and multi-site playbooks are structural advantages independents must explicitly counteract.
