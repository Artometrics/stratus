CMS TEAM vs. CJR-X: What's the Difference and Which Hospitals Are Affected?

TEAM is live and regional; CJR-X is proposed and nationwide. Here is how the two mandatory bundled-payment models compare on episodes, risk, and timing.

Ahmed \"Eddie\" Qureshi, CEO
· June 17, 2026
TL;DR
  • TEAM is live since January 2026
  • CJR-X targets joint replacement
  • Scope and geography differ
  • Both push episode accountability

Two mandatory models now define Medicare’s surgical strategy. The first, CMS TEAM, is already operating. The second, the Comprehensive Care for Joint Replacement Expanded (CJR-X) model, was proposed on April 10, 2026 in the FY2027 IPPS proposed rule and would be the first nationwide mandatory episode model in Medicare history if finalized.

What is CMS TEAM?

TEAM covers five surgical episodes (LEJR, SHFFT, spinal fusion, CABG, major bowel) for hospitals in 188 selected Core-Based Statistical Areas (CBSAs), with a 30-day episode window, running January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2030. CMS lists 721 participants.

What is CJR-X?

CJR-X would expand the original CJR model (which ran 2016–2024) nationwide, covering only lower extremity joint replacement — hip, knee and, newly, ankle replacement — across a 90-day episode. CMS proposes an October 1, 2027 start. The public comment period closed June 9, 2026. Per McDermott+ and CMS, the agency cited the original CJR’s $112.7 million in net savings across performance years 6–7 as the basis that qualified the model for nationwide expansion.

CMS TEAM vs. CJR-X: side-by-side comparison

How do TEAM and CJR-X interact?

If finalized, hospitals in TEAM would be exempt from CJR-X for TEAM’s duration to avoid dual LEJR accountability under conflicting methodologies (30-day vs. 90-day). After TEAM concludes in December 2030, eligible hospitals would move into CJR-X.

Frequently asked questions

Is CJR-X final?

No. CJR-X is a proposed model as of the FY2027 IPPS proposed rule (April 10, 2026); a final rule is expected in late 2026.

Which model is broader, CMS TEAM or CJR-X?

CMS TEAM covers five procedures but only 188 selected markets; CJR-X would cover only lower extremity joint replacement but nationwide, affecting far more hospitals overall.

Can a hospital be in both CMS TEAM and CJR-X?

No. If CJR-X is finalized as proposed, TEAM participants would be exempt from CJR-X for the duration of TEAM.

What is the episode-length difference between CMS TEAM and CJR-X?

CMS TEAM uses a 30-day post-discharge episode window; the proposed CJR-X model uses a 90-day window.

When would CJR-X start?

CJR-X is proposed to begin October 1, 2027, if finalized.
